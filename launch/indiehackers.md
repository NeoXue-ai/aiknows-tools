# IndieHackers Launch Post

**Title:** I curated 121 AI tools and 53 directories — sharing the directory list free

**Body:**

Hey IH 👋

I just shipped **aiknows.tools** — a curated directory of 121 AI tools and 53 submission directories, all hand-picked and tagged.

**Why I built it:**

When I launched my last AI side project, I burned 3 days hunting for directories to submit to. Every "Top 100 AI Directories" list I found was either:
- 6+ months stale
- Full of dead links
- Listed with no DR (domain rating) or pricing data
- Pure SEO spam

So I built the resource I wished existed.

**What's in it:**

🔧 **121 AI tools** across 8 categories — writing, image/video, code, SEO, ecommerce, productivity, research, business. Every tool has pricing type, tags, and a verified badge.

📂 **53 submission directories** (the flagship page):
- DR (Domain Rating) for each
- Free / paid / both submission type
- Dofollow / nofollow link type
- Listing speed
- Direct submit URLs
- Niche focus

Top 5 by DR: Product Hunt (91), G2 (93), Capterra (92), SaaSHub (67), Futurepedia (62).

**How it makes money (it doesn't, yet):**

Right now it's free. I'll add a sponsored listings section once traffic picks up, and a "submit your tool" flow with featured placement is live now ($ — to be set).

**The interesting part:**

The directories page is built like a **launch checklist** — not a list. Each row tells you what you get (dofollow? listing speed?) and points to the submit URL.

Most "AI directory" sites feel like spam. I wanted one that respects your time.

**Stack:**

Next.js 16 + Supabase + Vercel. Open-source-ish, but the curation is the moat.

**What I'd love feedback on:**

1. **For builders here:** Does the directories page actually save you time, or is it missing the columns you need?
2. **For launchers:** Is the tool submission flow clear?
3. **Anyone want a free featured listing** in exchange for brutally honest feedback on the site? Comment with your tool and I'll DM.
