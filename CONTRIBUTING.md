# Submit or correct an AI tool

You can contribute through GitHub. You do not need an account on AIknows.

- [Submit a tool](https://github.com/NeoXue-ai/aiknows-tools/issues/new?template=tool.yml)
- [Correct an existing listing](https://github.com/NeoXue-ai/aiknows-tools/issues/new?template=correction.yml)

Sign in to GitHub, open the appropriate form, fill in the required fields, and submit the issue. Your submission is public, so include product information and public evidence only.

For a new tool, provide its name, official website, a short description, a full description, and the closest category. The slug is the short name used in the listing URL, for example `example-tool`. If you are unsure about a subcategory, leave it blank.

For a correction, provide the listing URL, the field that needs changing, the correct information, and a public evidence link.

An editor reviews each submission. Accepted tools go through a content PR and appear after review, merging, and the next site publication. Submitting an issue does not publish a listing automatically. Track progress in your issue and the linked PR.

For general questions, use [the contact page](https://aiknows-tools.vercel.app/contact).
